---
id: F251
title: "Mint Info stats/capabilities and Mint history states not yet seen on a device or covered by a native scenario"
priority: medium
needs: device
---

Mint Info's four-stat grid (24h uptime, Operation success, Probe latency, Operations sampled,
each labelled with its source), the "What this mint supports" capability and spending-fee pills,
and Mint history's three states (`mint-changes-waiting`, `mint-changes-none`, saved revisions with
`mint-changes-refresh-failed`, pull-to-refresh) were checked by type-check and Jest only; no
simulator or phone was available. Unseen: the grid's skeleton-to-value crossfade not shifting the
page as the longer source subtitles land, pill wrapping at narrow widths and large text, the
fee pill colours in light and dark, FlashList pull-to-refresh on both platforms, and the
background sweep's first run after a cold start not delaying the wallet screen. No native
harness scenario opens Mint history for a mint with no baseline, waits on
`mint-changes-waiting`, and leaves.

The ADR 0036 sentence "it adds no requests beyond the info fetches the app already makes" is
stricter than the sweep as built: the sweep asks each wallet mint for `/v1/info` once a day
(after a success) or on a 5 min .. 24 h backoff, which the app's own 24h identity refresh
mostly overlaps but does not replace. The owner decides whether the ADR or the sweep changes.

## Done when

Each item above has been seen working on an iPhone and on Android, with screenshots, a native
scenario enters Mint history and leaves, and the ADR and the sweep agree.
