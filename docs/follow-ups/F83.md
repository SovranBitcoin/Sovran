id: F83
title: "Deletion steps of delete-all and profile removal wait with no time limit"
priority: low
needs: decision
---

The steps of delete-all and of profile removal that delete things (wallet reset, secure storage, AsyncStorage, files) wait with no time limit, so a native call that never answers leaves the flow, and the lock, waiting with no message. They are left unbounded on purpose for now: giving up partway through a deletion without knowing whether it ran is worse than waiting. The disk guard, the write of the new active account, key derivation and the wallet close are bounded.

**Handed to the owner on 2026-10-12; no code changed.** A time limit is easy to add; what happens when it runs out
is the decision, because at that point the app does not know whether the deletion ran. (The read-only inspection
before a removal is now bounded on its own.) When a deletion step does not answer in time, which should it be?

- **A (recommended). Hold.** Keep the lock, stop on a screen that says the deletion did not finish and offers
  "Close the app and open it again", and let the next launch finish or refuse. Nothing else can run on
  half-deleted data. Needs the durable step record of F92 to finish the job on the next launch.
- **B. Report and release.** Say which step did not finish and let the person carry on. Simple, but the account
  may be half deleted while the app is in use.
- **C. Leave it unbounded.** A native call that never answers is rare; the cost is a screen that waits with no
  message until the app is restarted.

## Done when

Each deletion step has a bound and a stated outcome when it runs out (most likely a hold).
