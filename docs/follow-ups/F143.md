title: "On a first launch the splash covers the Terms page for nine seconds"
priority: medium

On a first launch the splash covered the Terms page for nine seconds: the page was ready after two, and the
splash waited out its eight-second fallback for the wallet home's QR button. Fixed on 2026-10-12: the pages
shown in place of the app (Terms, onboarding and the restore prompt from `AppGate`; key recovery and a stopped
account from the keys provider) now tell the splash so (`useShownInsteadOfWallet` in
`app/shared/lib/qrButtonAnchor.ts`), and it fades at once.

Seen working in the bundled test build on iPhone 17 Pro simulators, four at a time, by the app's own log: the fade
began at 2.6 s, `screen.mount LegalDocumentScreen` at 2.65 s, `splash.gone` at 3.7 s; the first-launch story's
first step went from 9.3 s to 3.4 s. `app/__tests__/splashUncoversGatePage.test.tsx` holds both halves: uncovered
within a second, and still an eight-second wait when no such page is shown.

The first-launch story now fails if Terms takes more than seven seconds to show. The key recovery and stopped
account pages have only their unit tests: neither was seen on a device.

A launch that opens on the wallet home was then seen to keep its morph, on iPhone simulators: the story
`app/e2e/stories/library/open-an-empty-wallet.json` passes only when the app logs `splash.gone` with `by: morph`.

Not yet seen running: Android, and a store build on a phone. `splash.gone` is logged 1.1 s after the first
`screen.mount`, a little over the second this entry first asked for, because the 750 ms fade is followed by a
timer on a busy start-up thread; the page is visible before that.

## Done when

On Android and on a phone, a first launch is seen to show Terms at once and a launch of a wallet that has finished
onboarding is seen to morph the splash into the QR button.
