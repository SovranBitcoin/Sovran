---
id: F143
title: "On a first launch the splash covers the Terms page for nine seconds"
priority: high
needs: investigation
---

Seen on 2026-10-12 playing the first end-to-end story (`app/e2e/stories/library/first-launch-accept-terms.json`)
on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator, in the bundled test build, on a fresh install. The app's own log, by its clock:

```
  333 ms  SplashMorph: init done but no anchor yet — waiting up to 8000ms
 1777 ms  screen.mount LegalDocumentScreen (content 171 ms)
 8347 ms  SplashMorph: no anchor — fading out
 9147 ms  splash.gone
```

The Terms page is mounted, laid out and accepts touches from 1.8 s (the story tapped through Terms and Privacy in
half a second underneath), while the splash stays over it until 9.1 s. `NativeSplashLayoutGate`
(`app/shared/blocks/NativeSplashLayoutGate.tsx`) holds the splash until the wallet home's QR button publishes an
anchor to morph into, and only gives up after eight seconds. On a first launch, and on any launch that opens on
something other than the wallet home, no such button will appear. The pull request for 0.1.4 already made the
splash fade at once when a blocking start-up stage fails; this is the same wait on a page that is not a failure.

Read in the source and in one simulator log. Not checked: a store build on a phone, Android, and which other
first pages wait the same way (the migration retry screen, the identity mismatch screen, the Welcome back page).

Left because when to stop waiting is a choice about start-up: the gate would have to know that the first page is
not the wallet home, or the pages that are not would have to say so.

## Done when

A first launch shows the Terms page as soon as it is ready, with `splash.gone` logged within a second of the first
`screen.mount`, and the wallet home still gets its morph.
