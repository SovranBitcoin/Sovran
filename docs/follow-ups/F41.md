id: F41
title: "The amount screen's Next menu uses a stale snapshot and can offer a rail with no mint"
status: open
priority: high
needs: fix
---

The amount screen's Next menu ("Select options") is gated on `entry.methodContext` — a SNAPSHOT of trusted mints, balances and the per-unit capability map taken once by `createAmountEntryMethodContext` when `enterAmount` navigated. Nothing refreshes it: `AmountFlowScreen`, `AmountSelector` and `screen-actions/session` never touch `methodContext`, `decorateEntry` is skipped for `amountEntry`, and availability is computed by the manager from the raw entry. So funds arriving, a mint being trusted/untrusted, or a capability map rebuilt for a new unit while the screen is open do not re-gate the rails, and the menu can offer a rail whose picker then finds no mint. The unit half of this now fails closed (`getMintMethodCapability` rejects a capability derived for another unit) and the churn that caused it is gone (`resolveActiveUnit`), but the snapshot is still a snapshot.

## Done when

The menu derives from the live wallet context, so a rail is offered iff the picker it opens has a candidate; a mid-flow balance or trust change re-gates it
