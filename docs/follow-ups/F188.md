title: "The logger still prints unredacted text when a transport throws or an error message is a bare hex key"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

In `app/shared/lib/loggerCore.ts`, a transport that throws has its error passed to `console.error` unredacted, and
`redactKnownSecretSubstrings` does not catch a message that is only a 64-character hex key. The mnemonic heuristic
also redacts some ordinary twelve-word sentences. Found by review of the nested-error redaction fix.

## Done when

Both paths are redacted, with tests, and the false positive is measured.
