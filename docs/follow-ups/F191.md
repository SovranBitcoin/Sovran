id: F191
title: "A long name or picture cached from a Nostr profile makes the whole account list load empty"
priority: high
needs: decision
---

`profile-store` keeps `cachedDisplayName` (512 characters at most) and `cachedPicture` (2,048) in a plain array
(`app/shared/stores/global/profileStore.ts`). The writer copies the kind-0 name and picture as they arrive, with no
limit, in every release from 0.1.0 and today. One value over the limit and the next start rejects the whole blob: the
app opens on account 0 alone, and the other accounts' rows stay on disk with nothing opening them. Keys and wallet
databases are untouched and the original bytes are kept under `profile-store:unreadable`, which nothing reads back.
0.1.3's reader has the same limits, so this is not new with the upgrade. Pinned in
`app/__tests__/releasedWriterBounds.test.ts`. Left because it changes how saved data is read. Tried in a throwaway
copy: `.catch(undefined)` on the two fields keeps the accounts and every upgrade test still passes.

## Done when

An over-long cached name or picture costs at most that one label, never an account row, and new writes are capped.
