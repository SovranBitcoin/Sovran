The baseline research-loss defect is corrected: accepted research, sourced observations and
unknowns enter later briefs, and sealed snapshots survive restart. New routed tasks require
pre-edit path history; final audit rechecks changed paths. Native routing includes the
superseding story ADR, and relevant component tests receive the reference for their declared
library major version.
Large reference documents remain available through verified snapshot pointers.

Remaining work concerns useful coverage, not the existence of those fields. Prove with real
workers that historical objections, deleted follow-ups and unresolved knowledge change the
implementation and survive repair/fallback. A cited commit or hash does not establish that a
worker understood it. Investigate associated PR discussions and the final merged patch;
record unavailable or partial history explicitly.

Research cannot add new context/reference paths or widen the sealed plan in place. A discovery
requiring different inputs currently needs a newly prepared task. Evaluate whether that is
sufficient for the initial intake experience before adding another mutable draft protocol.
Keep unrelated backlog outside task authority. See the
[handoff design](../research/full-send-reliability.md) and [implemented contract](../../harness/README.md).

## Done when

Research facts and unknowns survive sealing, restart and fallback. Changed behavior has
baseline history/rationale coverage or an explicit gap; important unexplained guard removal
cannot pass. Relevant docs, tests, follow-ups, structure and upstream obligations have a
check/artifact or justified non-applicability. Fresh review starts with original requirements
and raw evidence, and repair preserves findings without treating old receipts as passes.
Tests include an objection recorded only in a prior PR, a deleted follow-up whose bug is
reintroduced, a related prerequisite that must block, and unrelated backlog that must not
expand task authority. Reviews select applicable risk lenses and preserve unresolved
objections through fresh-review and repair rounds.
