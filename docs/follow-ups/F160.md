At baseline f12db054d, a public-controller probe submitted a unique research summary and
observation, sealed intake, then read the implementation brief: the stored facts were absent.
The draft cannot incorporate research into its context before sealing. Pre-edit history,
docs/follow-up/layout applicability and unresolved knowledge are mostly prompt guidance.

Use the [researched handoff design](../research/full-send-reliability.md), extending current
roles and reusing audit-commit's history procedure. Preserve authority snapshots, failed
repair logs and current-source guards; add sourced facts/decisions/unknowns and dispositions.
Historical investigation must precede behavior edits, with explicit uncertainty.
Include associated PR discussions and the final merged patch, later reversions, and relevant
open follow-ups. Closed follow-ups live in Git history: recover their reason and closing
change when the task may revive the failure. Record unavailable discussion as a gap.
Route current native ownership too: ADR 0033 supersedes the older running model, while
older scenarios still cover areas without a replacement story. The current context router
only names the older ADR; supplying it alone can steer workers toward obsolete assumptions.

## Done when

Research facts and unknowns survive sealing, restart and fallback. Changed behavior has
baseline history/rationale coverage or an explicit gap; important unexplained guard removal
cannot pass. Relevant docs, tests, follow-ups, structure and upstream obligations have a
check/artifact or justified non-applicability. Fresh review starts with original requirements
and raw evidence, and repair preserves findings without treating old receipts as passes.
Tests include an objection recorded only in a prior PR, a deleted follow-up whose bug is
reintroduced, a related prerequisite that must block, and unrelated backlog that must not
expand task authority. Reviews select applicable risk lenses and preserve unresolved
objections through fresh-review and repair rounds.
