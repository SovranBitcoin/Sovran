title: "Delete Account treats a refused deletion as complete"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

`DeleteScreen` ignores the `false` that `deleteAllProfiles` returns when the lock is busy, the disk guard refuses or
an early step fails: it logs completion, shows nothing, and the slider stays spent.
`app/__tests__/deleteScreen.test.tsx` pins this. Left because what the person should be shown is a wording and design
choice.

## Done when

A refused deletion tells the person nothing was deleted and lets them try again.
