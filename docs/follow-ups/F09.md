`apiClient` relaxes shared profile schemas locally. Reconcile with `@sovranbitcoin/schemas` and the producer.

**Looked at on 2026-10-12; the change belongs in two other repositories.** `app/shared/lib/apiClient.ts` extends
the shared schemas in four places because nagg sends things `@sovranbitcoin/schemas` does not describe: an
optional `score` on a top follower, optional and nullable counts on the full profile (`followers`, `follows`,
`nodes`), `minVersion` on the latest-version reply, and the rates reply. Two of the four already say so in a
comment beside them. To finish: add those shapes to `sovran-schemas`, confirm with nagg which are deliberate
(a field it may omit) and which are drift, release the package, then delete the local extensions here and bump
the dependency. Until then each extension should carry the one-line reason the other two have.
