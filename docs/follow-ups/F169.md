priority: medium
needs: fix

In `harness/src/controller.ts` a research assessment that fails or is rejected (for example a
pass verdict that carries blocking findings, or a sealed check the repository cannot run) ends
the task, so the coordinator cancels it and creates another. Seven of eleven tasks between
2026-10-15 and 2026-10-17 ended in research, and one change went through four contracts in
an hour and a half. Left because changing what sealing means needs its own design pass.

## Done when

Before sealing, research can return a typed contract amendment that the controller applies to
the same task and presents again for intake, keeping its history, and a test shows a task
whose first research found an unrunnable check reach sealing without being recreated.
