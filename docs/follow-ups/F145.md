id: F145
title: "The simulator screen capture in serve-sim 0.1.44 crashes about once in fifteen stories"
priority: low
needs: upstream
---

The end-to-end runner watches the simulator's screen through `SimCapture` in the `serve-sim` native addon
(`app/e2e/stories/screenFrames.ts`). On 2026-10-12 it ended the process holding it in 3 of 40 stories: a memory
fault on the addon's own `frame-capture` and `napi.encode` queues (an over-release in Swift), under Bun and under
Node alike, so it is the addon's and not the runtime's. The crash reports are in
`~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports/` (`bun-2026-10-12-2254*.ips`, `node-2026-10-12-2258*.ips`).

A story survives it: the capture runs in a process of its own that `screenWatcher.ts` restarts, and while no
watcher has a frame the screen is treated as changing. The cost is a picture taken a few hundred milliseconds
late on the step where it happens, and a story that fails if the watcher dies six times.

Left because the fix is in another project. `serve-sim` 0.1.48 was published on 2026-10-11 and has not been tried:
changing the version touches the lockfile every session shares.

## Done when

A run of a few hundred stories reports no watcher restarts (`screenWatcherRestarts` in each `report.json`), on a
newer `serve-sim` or with the fault reported upstream and fixed.
