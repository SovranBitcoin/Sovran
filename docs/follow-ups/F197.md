title: "Receive requests can point payers at relays Sovran does not read"
priority: medium
needs: investigation
---

A request's relay hints are the first three payment relays: `relay.vertexlab.io` first in 0.1.3 and today, and
`relay.8333.space` third in 0.1.3. Sovran reads gift wraps through nagg, Primal and the default relays, which include
neither. A request made by 0.1.3 keeps its hints after the upgrade until it rotates. The payer stops after the first
relay accepts, so the copy on a relay Sovran reads may never be written. Partly inferred: how nostr-tools closes, and
which relays nagg ingests, were not checked.

## Done when

Either the receiver reads every relay it hints, or it is shown that a payment sent to a hinted relay always reaches it.
