Asset provenance, cache retention, and per-surface image transitions (the 1 s default delays dense rows).

**Handed to the owner on 2026-10-12.** Two different things are in here.

1. **Where each image came from and under what licence** is not in the repository and cannot be worked out from
   the files. Someone who knows has to list them (a `SOURCES.md` beside the assets is enough): for each file or
   folder, the source and the licence, or "made for Sovran".
2. **The one-second fade on dense rows.** How long should an avatar or mint icon take to appear in a list?
   - **A (recommended). No fade in lists, the current fade elsewhere.** Rows stop looking as if they are loading.
   - **B. A short fade everywhere (about 150 ms).** One value, slightly softer than none.
   - **C. Leave it.**
   Whichever is chosen needs a look on a phone with a cold image cache.

## Done when

Source and license known; row images avoid long fades
