Same family as the redeem queue and the account list, for eight more stores. The writer saves a value with no limit
and the reader rejects the whole blob when it is over one: a swap leg's error text over 2,048 characters
(`swap-transactions-store`: every swap group); a contacts document over 65,536 characters or a tag over 2,048
(`nostr-social-store`: follows, likes, deleted notes, zap totals); an own note over 65,536
(`own-content-store`: every own note); a 65th relay (`nostr-relay-list-store`: every relay and the published
flag); a search over 2,048 (`search-history-store`); a mint name over 256, icon over 2,048 or description over 4,096
(`mint-metadata-store`: every mint); more than 1,000 albums or 10,000 wallpapers (`wallpaper-store`, not shown to
be X(`wallet-lifecycle`, no caller passes one); a location saved under an entry id over 256 characters
(`transaction-location-store`, no caller shown to produce one). True of 0.1.0 to 0.1.3 and of today's writers. The bytes are kept under
`:unreadable`. Each is pinned in `app/__tests__/releasedWriterBounds.test.ts`. Left because the repair changes how
saved data is read. A tolerant read for all of them was tried in a throwaway copy: every upgrade, capacity and
resilience test passed, and `persistSchemaDrift` needs its snapshots regenerated for the five stores that lose a
limit. That check cannot see a `.catch()` being added or removed.

## Done when

No single saved value can empty a store: the reader keeps what a release wrote or drops only that entry, and the writer applies the limit.
