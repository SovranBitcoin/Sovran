title: "A follow can wait for ever, and nothing on screen says it is not published"
priority: high
needs: decision
---

Since pass 2 a follow or unfollow is published only on top of the contact list that a complete read of every write
relay just returned, so it can no longer shrink the public list from a stale copy. The cost: it waits, showing
"Following" the whole time, when a write relay never answers, when nagg or Primal know a newer list than any relay
holds, when the newest list is malformed or dated in the future, when the relay-list discovery has not answered, or
when two lists from the same second cannot be compared. There is no "waiting to sync" indication. Open choices: show
the waiting state; after how many failed reads a dead write relay is dropped from both the read and the publish
(risk: a newer list that lives only on that relay is overruled). Two limits cannot be closed from this side: a relay
that answers "nothing" while holding a list it will not show, and another device publishing at the same moment. Not
covered: the mute list publishes with the old relay targeting and no relay-list readiness check; the signer signs
whatever kind 3 another app hands it (`app/features/nostrSigner/lib/methodHandlers.ts`).

## Done when

The owner has ruled on the waiting indication and the dead-relay rule; the mute list uses the same read-then-publish rules.
