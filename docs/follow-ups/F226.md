title: "An acceleration invoice that arrives after its screen closed is still handed back"
priority: low
needs: decision
---

`useMempoolAcceleration.requestInvoice` returns the BOLT11 to its caller even when the screen or transaction it was
for is gone, so the send screen's handler can still start a scan from a closed screen. Returning nothing instead
would show "Could not create the acceleration invoice". Left in pass 2 as a choice between the two.

## Done when

A late invoice starts no payment flow, with a test.
