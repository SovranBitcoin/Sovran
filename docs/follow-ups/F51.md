Native incremental Routstr streaming remains unverified. `parseSSEStream` uses `response.body.getReader()` when available and otherwise buffers `response.text()`. The SDK owns fetch; synthetic ReadableStream tests do not establish what native fetch exposes.

## Done when

Capture a successful native completion showing incremental chunks on both platforms, or deliberately adopt a streaming transport with equivalent timeout, cancellation and recovery behavior
