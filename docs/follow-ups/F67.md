status: open

Choosing a mint in the picker the amount screen opened now pops back to that amount screen with the new mint (`router.dismissTo`), instead of navigating forward and stacking a second amount screen over the picker (`app/features/send/lib/amountReturn.ts`, `enterAmount` in `sovranPaymentConfig.ts`). The rule reads the flow's own stack, so it applies on Android too, where the picker is a pushed page. Covered by unit tests of the rule and of both flows; the stack behaviour was checked against expo-router's reducer, not on a device.

## Done when

On iPhone and Android, in send and receive: type an amount, open the picker, choose another mint; the sheet or page closes onto the same amount screen with the typed amount kept and the new mint shown, one Back leaves the amount screen, and Next pays from the chosen mint. The picker as the first screen of a flow still goes forward
