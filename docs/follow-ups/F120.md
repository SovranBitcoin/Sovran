The semantic checks in `.jevlint/checks` replaced the earlier reviewer on 2026-10-12. Its 21
hand-written questions were carried over; its 115 selected protocol rules were not. They were 16
from the Bitcoin BIPs (bip39, bip32, bip43, bip321), 67 from the Nostr NIPs (nip01, nip04, nip06,
nip17, nip19, nip46, nip59, nip60, nip61, nip65) and 32 from the Cashu NUTs (nut06, nut10, nut11,
nut12, nut18), chosen because this repository, not a library, makes the decision each rule is
about. The list, the reason for each family and the rule text are in `hunch.config.ts` and
`hunch.lock` at commit `db09d694d`. Questions compiled from the skills and the contributor
conventions were dropped as well; the conventions themselves remain in `docs/review`.

They were left because a saved check is a search of its own, a few minutes and around a million
input tokens when first run, so 115 single-rule checks would cost more than they return. Which
should come back?

- **A (recommended). One check per protocol area the app implements itself**, about a dozen:
  mnemonic and seed handling, BIP-321 URIs, the NIP-46 signer, gift-wrapped messages, the NIP-60
  wallet and NIP-61 nutzaps, relay filters and subscriptions, DLEQ on offline receive, NUT-18
  payment requests, P2PK lock conditions. Each names the rules it stands for.
- **B. None.** The 33 checks already ask about money, keys and saved data, and the libraries own
  the protocol details.
- **C. All 115**, run only by family before a release.

## Done when

The owner has chosen, and the chosen checks exist in `.jevlint/checks` or this entry is deleted
