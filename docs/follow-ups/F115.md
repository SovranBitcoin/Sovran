---
id: F115
title: "Switch and Connect saves the pairing link, with its secret, in plain AsyncStorage"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

Choosing "Switch & Connect" restarts the app under another account, so `switchProfileAndPair`
(`app/features/nostrSigner/lib/switchProfileAndPair.ts`) hands the re-encoded `nostrconnect://` link to
`setPairingIntent`, and `app/features/nostrSigner/lib/pairingIntentStorage.ts` writes it to AsyncStorage. The link
carries the pairing secret. It is read once and expires, but until then it is on disk unencrypted, which the
secrets contract ([contracts](../review/contracts.md)) does not allow. Found by a Codex audit of commit c28742926
on 2026-10-11; it predates that commit.

Keep the link in SecureStore instead, under a new key, and remove the AsyncStorage row an older build may have
left. The stored shape is new data under a new name, so no old install has to read it.

## Done when

The pairing intent's link is never written to AsyncStorage, a leftover row from an older build is deleted on
launch, and Switch & Connect still opens the review after the restart, with tests for each.
