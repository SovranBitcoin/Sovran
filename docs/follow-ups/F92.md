Profile removal and delete-all keep no durable record of the step they reached. Killed after the wallet is deleted and before the list is saved, removal refuses on the next launch because the wallet is missing; delete-all leaves a mix of deleted and kept state.


**Looked at on 2026-10-12.** Removal is not offered in the app at present (F140); delete-all is. A removal runs after its checks pass (no balance, nothing pending) and the person
confirms. It then deletes, in this order (`profileRemovalPorts.prepare` in `app/shared/lib/profile/profileRemovalStorage.ts`): the
account's saved stores, its caches, its Nostr database, its keys and payment recovery records, its signer secrets,
its wallet database, and last its row in the account list. Nothing records which step was reached. If the app is
killed part-way, the account is still listed with some of its data gone: an imported account whose key was already
deleted cannot be opened again, and a later removal is refused because the wallet database is missing. Delete All
has the same shape (`deleteAllProfiles` in `profileSessionOrchestrator.ts`): wallet databases first, then keys,
then everything else.

A marker is new saved data (one key, added, nothing renamed), which is allowed. What it does at the next launch
is the owner's choice, because it deletes at start-up:

1. **Finish it at the next launch, without asking (recommended).** The person already confirmed, and the checks
   already passed; a half-removed account is worse than a removed one. The marker is written before the first
   deletion and cleared after the list is saved. At launch, before any account is opened, a marker for a removal
   runs the remaining steps for that account only, and a marker for Delete All runs the wipe again.
2. **Ask at the next launch.** "Removing this account was interrupted." with Finish removing and Not now. Not now
   leaves the account listed and unusable where its key is already gone, so the question returns each launch
   until it is finished.
3. **Leave it as it is and say why.** No marker. The refusal that the person meets today is reworded to say the
   removal was interrupted and that Delete All is the way out.

## Done when

A marker written before the first deletion lets the next launch finish the job.
