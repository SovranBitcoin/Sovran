`CURATED_FOR_YOU_PUBKEYS` in `nostr/src/facade/relay/for-you/curated.ts` seeds the For You feed with accounts whose
likes are pooled to find authors. The names record the intent; the hex keys were not checked
against the accounts they are meant to be, and a wrong key is a different person. The feed
module is switched off in the default build, which is why this was left.

## Done when

The owner has confirmed the list, and each key has been checked against the account's published
identity.
