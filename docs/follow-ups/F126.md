A review flagged 21 controls without a stable `testID` or an accessible label (`docs/review/sweep-ledger.md`, rule `actionable-control-testid`). Two were read on 2026-10-12 and are still true: the close action in `app/shared/ui/composed/FormSheetChrome.tsx` has neither, and the grant button in `CameraLayout.tsx` has no `testID`. The other 19 were never read and their locations were not kept, so they have to be found again (search for `Pressable` and `Button` without `testID` in `app/features` and `app/shared/ui`).

## Done when

Every pressable control on a registered page has a `testID` and an accessible label, checked by a lint rule or a test over `CANONICAL_PAGES`, so the list cannot go stale again.
