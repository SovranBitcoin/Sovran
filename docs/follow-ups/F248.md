title: "Privacy policy points to a Settings media page the app no longer has"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

The privacy policy in `copy/legal/documents.json` tells people to use "Settings → My media" to
delete their uploaded images. That page was renamed "Uploaded media" and has since been removed
from Settings altogether (ADR 0039, Wave 2 Settings pruning). The saved list of uploaded images
(`owned-media-store`) and the media server setting (`nostr-media-server-store`) are kept and
still cleared by Delete account, but there is no longer a page in the app to view or delete
uploaded images one by one.

The legal text was left unchanged because it is versioned and accepted by the person; how it
should now describe deleting uploaded images (another way in the app, a request to the media
server, or nothing) is the owner's call.

## Done when

The owner has reviewed `copy/legal/documents.json`, the privacy policy describes what the app
actually offers for uploaded images through whatever versioning the legal documents require, and
`bun run copy:lint` passes.
