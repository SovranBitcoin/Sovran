The Settings row and page that list uploaded Blossom images were renamed from "My media" to
"Uploaded media" (`SettingsScreen`, `SettingsMediaScreen`, the `(settings-flow)` media title).
The privacy policy in `copy/legal/documents.json` still tells people to use "Settings → My
media" to delete their images. It was left because legal text is versioned and accepted by
the person; changing it is the owner's call, not part of a navigation change.

## Done when

The privacy policy names the page as it appears in the app ("Settings → Uploaded media"),
through whatever versioning the legal documents require, and `bun run copy:lint` passes.
