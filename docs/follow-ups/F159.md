The controller currently seals a single provider/model per role, has no effort or attempt
lineage, and rejects implementation/review from the same provider. The owner approved
GPT-only completion during Claude quota exhaustion, with fresh reviewers, an additional
review by a different GPT model, and disclosure. That policy is not yet executable.

The dated [research and policy](../research/full-send-reliability.md) defines the candidate
routes, failure classes and limits. Implement a durable attempt transition, preserving
terminal-child reconciliation, source/context/generation identity, deadlines and old sealed
authority. Never reinterpret a negative review or ambiguous dispatch as availability failure.

## Done when

The policy is resolved and hashed at intake; explicit supported effort reaches T3. Quota
fallback dispatches exactly one eligible successor only after reconciliation. Restart and
late-result tests pass. Claude research or a failed review cannot waive extra review. GPT-only ready requires the additional different-model review with
no slot reuse and a model outside the full source-author set, records actual identities and discloses the fallback. Old tasks remain
readable without acquiring new permissions; unknown effective effort is not fabricated.
