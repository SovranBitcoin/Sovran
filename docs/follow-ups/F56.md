id: F56
title: "Timed locks wait on a coco refund-path release; cancel is wrongly offered on locked sends"
priority: medium
needs: upstream
---

Timed locks ("Reclaim after…") are withheld behind `P2PK_RECLAIM_ENABLED` (`wallet/src/p2pk/reclaimGate.ts`), and `@cashu/coco-core` ships unmodified ([ADR 0017](https://github.com/SovranBitcoin/Sovran/blob/main/app/docs/adr/0017-the-wallet-core-ships-unmodified.md)). Coco 2.0.0 refuses to roll back a pending P2PK send and signs a received proof with its `data` key only; upstream declares `canReclaim = false`. Separately, the transaction list offers cancel for any pending send (`isCancellablePendingEcash` ignores the lock), so on a locked send the tap reaches coco and is refused.

## Done when

A released coco signs the refund path; the gate is switched on without a patch; a timed lock is created, left unclaimed and taken back on a device against a live mint. The transaction list offers cancel by the same rule as the send screen
