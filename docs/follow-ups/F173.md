id: F173
title: "A Balance split in a unit other than sat reads sat balances, fees and proofs"
priority: high
needs: decision
---

`createRebalanceWalletPort` in `app/features/mint/lib/rebalanceWalletPort.ts` takes a unit but uses it only for the
mint receipt. Balances are read with no unit scope, the input fee and the melt fee probe use the sat wallet, and
`prepareBolt11MeltQuote` is called without a unit, so it quotes in sat. A split started in usd either skips every step
as low balance or spends the source's sats to mint usd at the destination. `app/__tests__/rebalanceWalletPort.test.ts`
pins this as it is. Left because there are two repairs and the choice is the owner's: carry the unit through all four
calls (Coco's `balances.byMint({ units })`, `getReadyProofs(mintUrl, filter)`, `getWallet(mintUrl, unit)`, and the melt
quote unit), or refuse a split in any unit but sat. Neither was run against a mint.

## Done when

A split in a non-sat unit either moves that unit's balance and prices fees in it, or cannot be started, and the test says which.
