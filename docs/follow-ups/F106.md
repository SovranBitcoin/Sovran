`ColadaAdapters` in `wallet/src/adapters/types.ts` declares seventeen adapters, threaded through
`wallet/src/react/ColadaProvider.tsx`. Ten are never read anywhere in `wallet/src` and have no implementation:
camera, haptics, notifications, ble, storage, secureStorage, qrEncoder, clock, random and logger. `imagePicker` and
`nostr` are read but never supplied by the app, so those paths are dead or silently do nothing. The shape is left
from when the package was a standalone library; it has one consumer now.

Found by the 2026-10-11 architecture survey, by search; not yet confirmed by deleting them and compiling.

## Done when

The unread adapters and their props are deleted, type-check and tests pass, and `wallet/README.md` no longer
describes them.
