Private message plaintext persists in AsyncStorage at **three** sites, not the one this entry used to name. (a) `createPubkeyScopedCache.ts` backs both `nip04Cache.ts` (NIP-04 plaintext) and `giftWrapCache.ts` (NIP-17 `UnwrappedDM`) — fix the factory, not the call sites. (b) `app/features/bitchat/stores/bitchatDmMessages.ts` persists `byPeer[].content` for BLE 1:1 messages marked `isPrivate: true`. Make plaintext memory-only, or design encrypted storage with retention. Blobs already written (`nip04-cache:v1`, `nip04-cache-neg:v1`, `nip17-unwrap-cache:v1`, `nip17-unwrap-cache-neg:v1`, and the bitchat DM store) hold plaintext on disk, so the change must also erase them.

## Done when

Safe key migration; no plaintext at rest at any of the three sites, including blobs written before the fix; offline and profile-isolation tests
