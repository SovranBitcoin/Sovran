Two Design System pages were added: `settings-design-system-ui-kit`
(`SettingsDesignSystemUiKitScreen`, every core component held in each state) and
`settings-design-system-scrubber` (`SettingsDesignSystemScrubberScreen`, five transitions held
at any frame). Both are registered in `CANONICAL_PAGES` and `PAGE_ROUTES`. Their controls carry
stable ids: `design-system-ui-kit-filter-<state>`, and `design-system-scrub-<transition>-<percent>`,
`-slider` and `-value`, plus the same under `design-system-scrub-all`. Jest tests cover
filtering, stops, "Scrub all" and slider frames (`designSystemScrubber.test.tsx`), and every
scenario has a structural snapshot.

What has not been done: no native JSON scenario enters, operates and leaves either page, and
neither page has been looked at running on an iPhone or an Android phone. It was left because
the work ran with no simulator or emulator. Points to check by eye:

- the `large-text` frame: a 62% column stands in for 160% type, because the app cannot set the
  phone's text size. Compare it with a real large-text setting.
- the `BalancePill` liquid tier on iOS 26. Snapshots pin only the flat tier.
- the house `Slider` driving a frame continuously without lag.

## Done when

A native scenario on both platforms opens each page from the Design System hub, picks one
filter and one stop (`design-system-scrub-all-50`), and waits on the selected state. Screenshots
of both pages, seen beside their neighbours, show nothing out of place.
