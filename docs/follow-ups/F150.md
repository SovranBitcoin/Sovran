id: F150
title: "Redeeming ecash from a link opened while Settings is open can leave the app redrawing forever"
priority: high
needs: fix
---

Found on 2026-10-13 by an end-to-end story on the iPhone simulator (bundled test build), not yet on a phone or
Android. With Settings open (drawer, Settings, P2PK Keys and back), a `cashu:` link is opened, the token page
(`ReceiveTokenScreen`) appears over Settings, and Redeem is pressed. The ecash is redeemed and the page shows
"Added to wallet", but in 5 of 9 plays the app then stopped responding for as long as it was watched (over 200 s):
the "Payment received" notice never left, the app logged nothing after 0.9 s, and it used a whole processor.

A sample of the process during the hang shows the JavaScript thread busy the whole time inside React's commit
(`UIManager::completeSurface`, `ShadowTree::commit`, Yoga layout), called from JavaScript again and again: a
render that never settles, not a wait on the mint. The last events logged were
`payment.copy_resolver.create`, `ui.screen.diff`, `tx.history_timeline.row_change` and `url.mint.normalize_key`.

It is not the lock: a plain token through the same path hung in 1 of 3 plays, and a token locked to the wallet's
key redeemed 3 times of 3 when Settings was closed first (`redeem-ecash-locked-to-my-key`, `redeem-ecash-from-a-link`
in `app/e2e/stories/library`). To see it, take `redeem-ecash-locked-to-my-key.json`, remove its "close Settings"
step, and play it a few times with `bun run e2e:story`.

Not looked into: which component renders without settling (the transaction timeline and the payment status notice
both draw then), and whether any other page open under the token page does the same.

## Done when

The story without its "close Settings" step passes ten times in a row, and it is then added to the library as it
is.
