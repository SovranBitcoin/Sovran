`wallet/docs/STATE_MACHINE.md` section 11 lists behaviours the payment machine does not have. Read against the code on 2026-10-12, thirteen are still true. Each changes what a person sees, so which to build is the owner's call:

1. The proof picker cannot say it appeared because the mint is offline (`chooseProofs` data has no reason; `resolveNext.ts` keys on `ctx.offline`).
2. A payment request cannot be paid while its mint is offline, and nothing warns the payer.
3. A single scanned payment request is never marked unavailable when no mint can pay it (`wallet/src/intent.ts` annotates only when there is more than one option).
4. Fiat sends pick the highest in-window amount, not the fewest proofs (`composeFiat` in `wallet/src/offline.ts`).
5. A payment request above the balance errors instead of offering a rounded-down amount (`amountFallback.ts`).
6. A failed ecash option gets no fallback to the other options, and fallback rows are not re-checked (`createMachine.ts`).
7. A selected mint that cannot cover the amount forces a trip to the mint picker instead of moving to the next mint that can (`resolveNext.ts`).
8. A failed Lightning payment never offers to pay the same invoice from another mint.
9. After an NFC ecash send fails, the tag's Lightning invoice is not tried.
10. NFC cannot overpay slightly from exact local proofs to complete a tap offline.
11. Onchain receive: amount enforcement, steering to a payment request, and showing the confirmation wait are not confirmed built.
12. Reviewing an unknown mint offers no "don't trust, move it to a mint I trust" path.
13. `isMintValidForFlow` is written twice, in `resolveNext.ts` and `contextResolution.ts`, so the two paths can disagree after a rule change. This one is not a product choice and can be fixed without asking.

Four gaps in that section no longer exist and are still to be struck from it: the local exact send after a send
failure (`shouldCreateLocalTokenFirst` in `effects.ts`), kind-0 profile resolution (the app injects it), the
candidate list seeded without `allowedMints` (`contextResolution.ts` passes it), and onchain being operationally
off (`mint-capabilities.ts` enables it).

**Question for the owner.** Which of 1 to 12 should be built?

1. **The three that stop a payment failing (recommended): 6, 8 and 9.** A failed payment offers the other way to pay.
2. **Those plus the offline ones: 1, 2 and 10.**
3. **None for now.** The section stays as a description of how the machine behaves.

## Done when

The owner has chosen; each chosen behaviour has its own fix entry naming the file and symbol, and section 11 keeps only what is deliberately not built.
