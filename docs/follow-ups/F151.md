Found on 2026-10-13 by an end-to-end story on the iPhone simulator. `app/app.json` registers the schemes
`lightning`, `lnurl`, `lnurlw`, `lnurlp` and `bitcoin` with the phone, so iOS offers Sovran for those links. But
the deep-link effect in `wallet/src/react/ColadaProvider.tsx` accepts only `cashu` and the app's own `sovran`
(`customSchemes` in `app/features/send/providers/Colada.tsx`): for `lightning:<invoice>` and
`bitcoin:<address>?amount=…` it logs `deepLink.ignored` with `scheme_not_accepted`, the router logs
`nav.unknown_route`, and the person is left on the not-found page with a "go to wallet" button. A `cashu:` link
works (`redeem-ecash-from-a-link` in `app/e2e/stories/library`).

Left because it is a choice about payments, not a slip: either the wallet scans what those links carry, as it does
for a pasted invoice or address, or the app stops registering schemes it does not take. Sibling wallets were not
surveyed. Android was not tried.

## Done when

Opening a `lightning:` link and a `bitcoin:` link each either starts the matching Send flow or is no longer
offered to Sovran, on both platforms, with a story for whichever it is.
