id: F149
title: "End-to-end money stories need a mint of their own, and three driver gaps closed"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

The stories in `app/e2e/stories` that move money use the public test mint. That has three costs:

- A saved wallet cannot be gone back to and spent from twice (the mint has signed its secrets, see F146), so
  money stories must form one line, each starting where the last ended, and cannot be tried again without a new
  wallet.
- A run depends on a server nobody here controls.
- Two workers could not share a wallet even if they wanted to.

`app/e2e/stories/localMint.ts` starts a private mint in 0.15 s that pays its own invoices in 20 ms, but the app
accepts only `https://` mint addresses (`normalizeUrlForApi` in `app/shared/lib/url.ts`, `fetchMintInfo` in
`app/shared/lib/apiClient.ts`, the wallet's `parse.ts`). Wanted: the private mint behind TLS with a certificate
the simulator trusts (`simctl keychain add-root-cert`), its database reset with the wallet's state.

Driver gaps found on 2026-10-13:

- Controls only iOS draws (the tab bar, menus, alerts) are found through the simulator's accessibility tree, which
  takes about a second a look and two looks a tap: 6 s for a tab change with four simulators running. The first
  tab-bar tap of `browse-the-tabs` was also lost in one of four parallel runs.
- `nav.action`, logged when a page change is asked for (`app/shared/lib/loggerScreen.ts`), never appears in the
  bundled test build, so "still" relies on a one-second allowance after a page appears.
- Not built: scroll until a control is visible, pasting (iOS asks permission), long press, the camera, and the
  Android adapter. `e2e/artifacts/stories` keeps every simulator's copy of a starting state's evidence.

## Done when

Money stories run against a mint the run starts and resets, a tab change takes under a second, and the list above
is empty or split into its own entries.
