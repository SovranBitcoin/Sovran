Built on 2026-10-13: `app/e2e/stories/privateMint.ts` gives each simulator two mints of the run's own behind TLS
it trusts, saved and put back with the wallet's state. Receiving, an on-chain send and a swap between two mints
have stories on them. Still to do there:

- The eight money stories on the public test mint still form one line (F146). They could move to the private
  mint, keeping one or two on the public one as proof of the real path.
- No story makes and redeems a locked token.
- The pretend mint never refuses or delays, so nothing plays a payment that fails or stays pending.

Driver gaps found on 2026-10-13:

- Controls only iOS draws (the tab bar, menus, alerts) are found through the simulator's accessibility tree, which
  takes about a second a look and two looks a tap: 6 s for a tab change with four simulators running. Just after a
  simulator boots that tree can answer "no frontmost application"; the runner looks again until the step's deadline.
- `nav.action`, logged when a page change is asked for (`app/shared/lib/loggerScreen.ts`), never appears in the
  bundled test build, so "still" relies on a one-second allowance after a page appears.
- Seen failing now and then with four simulators running, each in about one run in three of the 32 stories: a
  250-character invoice typed key by key losing a character; a tap just after a sheet opens not reaching the app
  (the sheet reports its slide as over in 0.2 ms, before it has moved). Putting text on the simulator's clipboard
  with `simctl pbcopy` and pressing Paste in Send did nothing, so long text is still typed.
- With 70 stories on four simulators, four full runs on 2026-10-13 passed 68, 69, 69 and 65, while the Mac was
  also busy with other work (load average over 30 with no simulator running). What failed changed from run to run:
  a look for the tab bar that found nothing for 15 to 40 s on one simulator, a tap just after a sheet opened, and
  a list scrolled a little further than the story assumed. None failed when played alone.
- The developer rows "Mock Fail Send" and "Mock Fail Melt" do not reach real payments: with each on, a real send
  and a real Lightning payment went through.
- A full wallet recovery left the app unresponsive for 8.7 s at one point (`recover-a-wallet-from-its-mints`).
- Not built: scroll until a control is visible, long press, the camera (no story presses Scan; the simulator has
  none), NFC, and the Android adapter. No story turns on the developer toggles that make a send or a payment
  fail (Settings, version row pressed three times), and the default build has no AI pages to play.
- A story that starts a profile restarts the app; the runner's link reconnects, but the app's clock starts again,
  so that step's app-clock times in the report mean nothing. `e2e/artifacts/stories` keeps every simulator's copy of a starting state's evidence.
