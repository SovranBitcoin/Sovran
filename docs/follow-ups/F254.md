ADR 0037 says the rebalance engine calls a `SingleLegTransferPort`. What exists is the reverse:
`createEngineSingleLegTransferPort` (`wallet/src/rebalance/singleLegPort.ts`) is built on the
engine's own one-leg path (`executeTransfer` with `directOnly`), and
`createCocoSingleLegTransferPort` (`app/features/mint/lib/rebalanceWalletPort.ts`) binds it to a
Coco manager. The seam has the Coco v3 `manager.ops.mintSwap` shape and hides the child quote and
melt ids, but `transfer()` and `runHop()` in `engine.ts` still compose mint quotes and melts
directly, and plan runs do not go through the port.

Left because inverting it moves the melt sequencing, fee headroom and recovery rules of
`transfer()` behind the port in one step, on money-moving code, and needs the engine's 57 tests
re-pointed at a port fake first.

## Done when

`engine.ts` runs every direct leg and every hop through a `SingleLegTransferPort` it is given,
the Coco 2.0.0 implementation of that port holds what `transfer()` does today, and the engine's
tests pass against a fake port without changes to what they assert.
