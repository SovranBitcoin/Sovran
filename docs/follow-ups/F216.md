The stale-edit defect is repaired on `surgery/decisions-1`: publication owns an immutable review,
checks the invocation/account/signer across awaits, and never stamps a later edit as published.
First-run publishing requires complete independent relay reads and refuses a timeout. Regression
tests cover edits during signer lookup, signing and acceptance, plus account changes.

Native confirmation of those ownership guards remains unrun on both platforms; F246 records the
controlled-fixture and device work. The separate unknown-relay discovery limitation is in F240.

## Done when

The concurrent edit and incomplete-read cases are exercised through the native flow on both
platforms without publishing a public event, and no later edit is erased or marked sent.
