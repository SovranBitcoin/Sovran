request behind it, and Retry does not restart a failed social read. `MintDistributionScreen.tsx` with
`mintDistributionStore.ts`: removing a mint while Split evenly is on leaves uneven saved weights under a switch that
says even. `.surgery/pass-2/surveys/transactions-wallet-mint.md` defects 3 and 5 (traced). The second writes saved weights through
existing actions.

## Done when

A replaced or failed social read ends in a result or an error that Retry clears; Split evenly is either true of the saved weights or off.
