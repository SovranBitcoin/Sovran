priority: low

In `features/settings/screens/SettingsEditProfileScreen.tsx`, the "Use my npub.cash address"
`CapsuleButton` and the "Previously used" choice chips are still `height={32}`, below the 44pt
touch target. The "Check again" and "Use my Sovran name" buttons beside them were raised to 44 in
the Wave 2E review fixes. These two were left as they were because that review named only those
two. `CapsuleButton` takes no `hitSlop`, so the fix is either `height={44}` or a `hitSlop` prop on
`CapsuleButton`. For the wrapping chip row, the second keeps the row compact.

## Done when

Every tappable capsule on Edit Profile has a touch target of at least 44pt, by height or slop, and
the row of previously used values still wraps without overlapping.
