The mint page now opens from the Select Mint list's three-dots button without waiting for the
mint's info (`mintSelector.getInfo` in `wallet/src/screen-actions/defaultHandlers.ts`), keeps its
rating stars and the ring around its icon on screen from the first frame (`MintInfoScreen`,
`RatingBarChart`'s `empty` state), and replaces the footer's Close with a Details button that
opens the shared Details modal (`buildMintDetailsRows`). Unit and source tests cover each part,
but none of it was looked at on an iPhone or an Android phone, and no native scenario or story
was run:

- that the page appears at once from the list, with the name and icon from the row, and fills in
  the rest without the layout jumping;
- that an unrated mint shows its stars marked "No ratings yet" at the same height as a score, and
  that the ring is drawn with the content rather than after it;
- that the Details modal stacks over the mint-flow modal on iPhone and pushes inside Android's
  flow (the open question of [F65](F65.md)), and reads well with every group filled;
- that the edited scenarios and stories still pass: they now wait for `mint-info-details-open`,
  and `read-about-the-test-mint` closes the page with `flow-header-close`, which was read from
  `app/config/flowLayoutOptions.tsx` (the mint page is the first screen of its flow), not seen.

The full-send controller has no native evidence to bind these to ([F156](F156.md)).

## Done when

On both platforms: the mint page opens from the Select Mint list with no wait, an unrated mint
shows its stars and a plain ring on the first frame, Details opens the modal with the mint's
groups and returns, and the `mint-info-*` scenarios and the three mint stories pass.
