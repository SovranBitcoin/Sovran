The claim of redux-era ecash (ADR 0032) has never run on a phone or against a real mint, and its prompt has no JSON E2E scenario because the harness cannot seed a `persist:SOVRAN` row. Three things are unverified: Coco's receive accepting 2024-era proofs passed as a token object (legacy base64 keyset ids in particular), the mint's NUT-07 answer for proofs of a retired keyset, and the prompt on iOS and Android. A group given up on (`could-not-claim`) has no retry in the UI; a declined one reopens only by adding the mint. Finding the proofs a mint rejects costs a few rolled-back receives in the wallet's history.

## Done when

A fixture row with real proofs from a test mint is claimed on a device; a scenario opens, declines and accepts the prompt on both platforms; Settings lists what was claimed and what was left, with a retry
