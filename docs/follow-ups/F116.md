title: "A swap step that depends on money still settling is retried or skipped as if it had not been sent"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

Found by a Codex audit of the settling change (commits f2fd1ad01 and a51e7e6d0) on 2026-10-11, both reproduced
with mocked wallet boundaries; both are older than those commits. In `wallet/src/rebalance/engine.ts`:

- **A paid middleman hop becomes retryable.** When hop A to M pays but M has not issued within the hop's wait,
  the next hop M to B runs anyway and fails for lack of balance. The chain is reported incomplete and the
  original transfer failed, so retrying it pays A to M a second time.
- **A dependent plan step is skipped for good.** When step A to M ends settling, the next step M to B starts
  half a second later, finds M short and is skipped. When the money does arrive nothing resumes it, and a retry
  leaves skipped steps out.

Both need the engine to know that a step is waiting on money in flight: hold the dependent hop or step until
the earlier one's receipt arrives, and end the run as still settling if it does not, without marking anything
failed or skipped.

Read again on 2026-10-12, which lowers the priority: neither case loses money. A swap moves ecash between the
person's own mints, so a hop that paid and whose next hop did not run leaves the amount in their wallet at the
middleman mint. What is wrong is where it ends up and what the screen says:

- `routeThroughMiddlemen` tries the next candidate route after any incomplete chain, including one whose first
  hop already paid, so more leaves the source while the first amount sits at the middleman.
- `releaseTrust` runs as soon as the chain returns. A middleman whose money is still on its way has no balance
  yet, so it is untrusted without the "stranded" notice, and the money then arrives at a mint the wallet no longer
  lists.

So the change is: a hop whose receipt has not arrived ends the chain as settling (no next hop, no next
candidate, the transfer's outcome is settling), and the middleman it is arriving at stays trusted until the
receipt is in.

## Done when

A hop or step whose source is still settling is neither run nor skipped nor made retryable while that money is
in flight, a middleman that money is arriving at stays trusted, with a test for the middleman chain and one for two dependent plan steps, and no path pays the same
hop twice.
