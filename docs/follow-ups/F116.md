title: "A swap step that depends on money still settling is retried or skipped as if it had not been sent"
priority: high
needs: fix
---

Found by a Codex audit of the settling change (commits f2fd1ad01 and a51e7e6d0) on 2026-10-11, both reproduced
with mocked wallet boundaries; both are older than those commits. In `wallet/src/rebalance/engine.ts`:

- **A paid middleman hop becomes retryable.** When hop A to M pays but M has not issued within the hop's wait,
  the next hop M to B runs anyway and fails for lack of balance. The chain is reported incomplete and the
  original transfer failed, so retrying it pays A to M a second time.
- **A dependent plan step is skipped for good.** When step A to M ends settling, the next step M to B starts
  half a second later, finds M short and is skipped. When the money does arrive nothing resumes it, and a retry
  leaves skipped steps out.

Both need the engine to know that a step is waiting on money in flight: hold the dependent hop or step until
the earlier one's receipt arrives, and end the run as still settling if it does not, without marking anything
failed or skipped.

## Done when

A hop or step whose source is still settling is neither run nor skipped nor made retryable while that money is
in flight, with a test for the middleman chain and one for two dependent plan steps, and no path pays the same
hop twice.
