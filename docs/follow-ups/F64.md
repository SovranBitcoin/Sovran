title: "Payment timeline cannot say why a mint has not credited or tell unreachable from slow"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

The payment timeline is add-only and treats the mint as the authority on crediting, with our explorer as a hint ([ADR 0027](../../app/docs/adr/0027-a-timeline-only-adds-rows.md)). What it still cannot say: *why* a mint has not credited a confirmed deposit (the mint's `min_amount` and the quote expiry do not reach the model, so the warning names both causes); an unreachable mint as distinct from a slow one; replacement as distinct from disappearance; a second payment or partial issue on a reusable quote; melt fee, change and proof. Rows drawn from explorer data are never taken back, so a tick can outlive the observation behind it; lock details arriving late can change the flow under a drawn card; and cancellation, restore failure and already-spent are all recognised by matching coco's error text, because coco exposes no structured reason. The thresholds are judgement calls (three blocks past the requirement, two minutes in flight, one minute reversing). The animation has not been reviewed on a device.

## Done when

The model is given `min_amount`, quote expiry and per-output deposit amounts and names the actual cause; mint reachability is an input; thresholds are confirmed against real mints; the Design System Timeline page plays every path on iPhone and Android with no row fading out
