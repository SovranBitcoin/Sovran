The redesign removes the overlapping carousel that intercepted Get Started (seen on an iPhone
17 Pro simulator on 2026-10-12). Cards now move only through Next and Back,
with Get Started in the shared footer on the last card. The body scrolls independently of the
footer. The design-system prompt preview uses the same screen and sample recovery actions.

On 2026-10-16, a disposable iPhone 17 Pro simulator completed Terms, Privacy, all four cards,
Back, Next, and one Get Started tap into the empty wallet. The onboarding preview then opened
from its deep link after setup, and Next and Back worked there. The sample recovery form opened above the preview without
showing real recovery information. All four cards were inspected
in dark appearance; screenshots are local ignored artifacts. Both Metro platform bundles were
built. Focused preview, timeline and visual-activity tests passed.

The maximum iOS text-size trial still showed the paragraph ending partway through its last
line before pagination. Natural-height scrolling replaces the zero-basis flex body, but this
trial has not established that every word remains visible; investigate native text measurement
and repeat after a fresh launch. Do not count maximum text size as passed.

Still unverified: Android native execution (no attached device or emulator), small-screen layout,
VoiceOver/TalkBack operation, physical-device animation feel, system reduced
motion, and signer-demo foreground/background behavior on devices. In ordinary Android harness runs,
check that decorative animation does not prevent UIAutomator from becoming idle: the removed
progress animation had an Android E2E guard, while strict captures already disable motion. The fresh-onboarding
scenario covers Back, and `preview-the-onboarding-cards` covers entering and leaving the preview.
The final iOS swipe trial found the horizontal card gesture competing with vertical scrolling;
that optional gesture was removed, leaving explicit navigation controls.
These JSON scenarios have not been executed as complete native harness runs in this session.

The saved semantic check run stalled on its first retrieval and was interrupted after several
minutes: zero of eight searches completed. Its questions were reviewed against the changed code,
including the preview menu host and navigation data, but automated retrieval remains incomplete.
