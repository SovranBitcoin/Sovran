title: "On the onboarding slides, Get Started shows but does not respond until the slides are lifted"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

Seen on 2026-10-12 on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator while writing the end-to-end story that builds the `onboarded`
state (`app/e2e/stories/states/onboarded.json`). After the legal pages the onboarding slides appear with the lower
half of the Get Started button showing beneath the slide card, its words readable, and "I have a recovery phrase"
below it. A tap on the part of Get Started that shows does nothing a person would expect: the app received the
touch (`ui.touch` in the log) and `onboarding.completed` was never logged. The slide card's gesture area lies over
the button, so the tap is taken as "next slide". The button answers only after the card has been swiped up, which
nothing on the page suggests. The old end-to-end scenario got through by tapping "Get Started" up to four times
until the wallet appeared (`app/e2e/scenarios/capture-onboarding.json`).

Where: `OnboardingScreen` in `app/features/onboarding/components/OnboardingScreen.tsx`. The button is drawn
before the carousel, and the carousel's `GestureDetector` covers it while the card is down
(`TOP_CAROUSEL_OFFSET`). The story's picture `05-confirm-and-leave-the-legal-pages.jpg` shows the page.

Read in the source and seen in one simulator run. Not checked: other screen sizes, where the card may cover the
button entirely, and Android.

Left because how the page should behave is a design choice: hide the button until the card is lifted, let a tap
on it through, or lift the card after the last slide.

## Done when

On the onboarding slides either a tap on a visible Get Started finishes onboarding, or Get Started is not shown
until it can be pressed; and the `onboarded` story no longer has to swipe first if the first was chosen.
