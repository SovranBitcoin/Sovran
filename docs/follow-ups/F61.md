status: open

A cross-mint swap reports its leg `done` when the destination mint has not issued within 15 s (`wallet/src/rebalance/engine.ts`, `waitForReceipt`), and an unresolved melt is persisted as `melting` because the stored leg status has no pending or unknown value (`app/shared/stores/profile/swapTransactionsStore.ts`). The UI therefore shows "Done" or "sending" for money whose outcome is not yet known. Add a persisted settling/unknown leg state and reconcile it from coco's mint and melt operations. The timeline's own claims moved to F64.

## Done when

A swap leg whose destination mint has not issued within 15 s, or whose melt is unresolved, is persisted and shown as settling or unknown, never as Done or sending. It moves to its final state once coco's mint or melt operation resolves, with a test.
