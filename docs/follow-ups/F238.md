Found by Codex reviews of the repair commits on `surgery/pass-2`; source-traced, not run. Money first.
(1) An older NFC tap that finishes its read late can still WRITE its token into a newer tap's tag session: the
machine checks tap ownership when releasing, not before `writeToken` (`wallet/src/machine/createMachine.ts` about
line 2003), so tap A's token can reach tap B's terminal. (2) `rollbackSend` reports `reclaimed` when Coco resolved
the reclaim but skipped the swap because fees would consume the amount, so a small token is called cancelled while
its proofs are still spendable by whoever holds it (`wallet/src/operations/defaultOperations.ts` about line 1323;
Coco `dist/index.js` about line 10636). (3) A reset during the tag write that then fails leaves the token out with
nothing telling the person; an ordinary failed write (no reset) is still rolled back although the terminal may have
received it. (4) A swap route counts as replacing a failed transfer from saved hop amounts, which the engine does not
update when it shrinks an invoice, and against a fee budget (2%, at least 5 sats per hop) that real fees can exceed:
a short delivery can read finished and a full one failed; one large route can also stand in for several failures
(`app/shared/lib/cashu/swapGroupPhase.ts`). (5) A payment-pending swap leg whose melt Coco holds as `init` cannot be
cancelled and stays pending for good once Coco deletes it at startup. (6) The melt and payment-request amount
screens still do nothing under a settled preview (restarting there would pay the same invoice again; needs a
decision). (7) An NFC scan error is shown or hidden by whoever holds the reader when it is reported, not by the scan
that failed.

## Done when

Each has a failing test and a repair, or an owner's ruling; an old tap never writes into a newer session; `reclaimed` means the mint invalidated the token.
