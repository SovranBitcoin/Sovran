An install that began on 0.0.45 to 0.0.56 and already passed through 0.0.62 to 0.1.3 had its per-account stores stranded under bare keys. Closed for the common case on 2026-10-10 by the `adopt-leftover-bare-stores-v1` migration: a leftover is given to account 0 when that account is the stored phrase's first account and has nothing of its own under that store's key. Still open: where the account has saved its own copy since, the leftover is kept on disk but not merged, so a Routstr key left there is not offered back.

## Done when

A leftover with a credential the account lacks is merged or shown, with a test.
