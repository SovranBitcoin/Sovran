title: "Bound native runs rebuild both platforms from scratch on every verify and repair"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

`playBound` in `app/e2e/stories/bound.ts` calls `ensureNativeBuild(platform, 'force', 'bundled')`,
so every native verification, and every repair after it, runs a full `xcodebuild` and Gradle
build: tens of minutes and several gigabytes per worktree. It is deliberate: the build folder is
ignored by Git, so a stamp left there could vouch for a binary the candidate did not produce.
`ensureNativeBuild` already refreshes only the JavaScript when the native fingerprint is
unchanged. Left because trusting a cached native part is the owner's call.

## Done when

The owner decides whether a native part keyed by its fingerprint, held outside the candidate's
checkout and hash-checked, may be reused with fresh JavaScript; then bound runs either reuse it
or this entry records why they never will.
