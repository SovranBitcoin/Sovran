title: "A held swap step cannot be resumed from the screen, and swap history shows an unfinished chain as complete"
priority: medium
needs: device
---

The rebalance engine now holds a step whose source is still being delivered to, instead of running or skipping
it (`stillArriving` in `wallet/src/rebalance/engine.ts`), and stops a chain at a hop whose money has not arrived.
A Codex review on 2026-10-12 found what the app then shows and offers:

- A held step stays `pending` and the run ends. `MintRebalancePlanScreen.tsx` offers Retry only when a step
  failed, so once the money arrives there is nothing to press to send the held step on.
- Swap history has no leg for a held step. A chain routed by hand can therefore read "Completed" after its first
  hop arrives although the onward hop never ran (`reconcileSwapLegs.ts`, `SwapTransactionRow.tsx`), and
  `SwapTransactionScreen.tsx` says "Complete" straight away while the toast correctly says the swap is still
  settling.
- The original step of an automatic chain is shown skipped on the plan screen while its history leg stays at
  melting, and the reconciler can later label that abandoned direct attempt failed.
- A hop that paid and arrived at a middleman, followed by a hop that failed for good, ends the transfer as failed
  with the amount at the middleman (the stranded notice says where). Pressing Retry on that transfer sends the
  amount from the source again. The engine no longer tries another route by itself in that case; the retry is the
  person's, and nothing warns them.

Left because each is something on screen and in history, to be looked at running on both platforms with a mint
that is slow to issue.

## Done when

A swap with a step waiting on money in flight says so on the plan screen and in history, offers a way to continue
once the money is in, never reads as complete while a step has not run, and warns before a retry that would send
an amount already sitting at a middleman.
