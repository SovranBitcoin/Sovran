`heroui-native` and `@gorhom/bottom-sheet` were replaced by house primitives
(`app/shared/ui/composed/Sheet.tsx`, `ToastHost.tsx`, `ListGroup.tsx`, `ActionMenuButton`'s
`MenuPopover`, `app/shared/ui/primitives/{PressableFeedback,PillButton,Field,Slider,Switch}.tsx`),
and `ProfileTierRing` moved from Skia to react-native-svg filters. They were checked in Jest only:
no simulator or emulator was available where the change was made. Things to look at on both
platforms: a sheet slides up with its content already drawn; the action-menu footer rides above
the keyboard (Import Nostr nsec form); a long footer list scrolls, and dragging down from its top
dismisses; the send-memo sheet and NFC sheet over route modals; toasts stack at the top above
sheets, swipe up to dismiss, auto-hide; the iOS menu popover sits above its button; sliders drag
and tap with VoiceOver/TalkBack adjust; the switch track colours; the tier ring's glow and blur
(react-native-svg `FeGaussianBlur`) on a Gold profile.

## Done when

Each item above was seen working on an iPhone and an Android phone (a short clip or screenshots),
and the action-menu, popup-sheet and toast native scenarios pass on both platforms.
