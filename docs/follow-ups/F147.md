priority: medium
needs: investigation
---

Measured on 2026-10-13 by the end-to-end runner while adding `testnut.cashu.space` from the Add Mints page: the
app did not answer the runner for 2.8 to 3.0 s in four of seven runs, during the restore that follows
`mint.add.item.added` (`runMintImport` in `app/features/mint/hooks/useMintImport.ts`, the call to
`manager.wallet.restore`). The runner's question is answered from the JavaScript thread, so touches were not
being handled either; the "Adding mint" spinner keeps turning because it runs off that thread. The whole step
takes 7 to 8 s.

Not checked: where in the restore the time goes (deriving and unblinding a batch of secrets is the likely work),
a phone, and Android.

Left because it is a measured observation from a simulator, not yet a diagnosis.

## Done when

Adding a mint keeps the app answering touches throughout (no gap over about 250 ms in the story's report), or the
work is shown to be unavoidable and is moved off the thread that handles them.
