id: F140
title: "Removing one profile is not offered: its account number can be handed out again, and the wording does not say what goes"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

The owner took single-profile removal out of the app on 2026-10-12. Tapping a profile in the switcher
switches to it again. The flow that called the removal (`removeInactiveProfile` in
`profileSessionOrchestrator.ts`, which took the transition lock and the disk guard) went with the menu, the
confirmation and their wording. The removal itself (`removeProfileData` in `removeProfile.ts`, the checks and
steps in `profileRemovalStorage.ts`, ADR 0030) is kept, with its tests, for when it returns. Delete Account is the
only way to remove anything.

Three things were wrong with it as it shipped to the branch:

1. **A removed profile's account number is handed out again.** `getNextAccountIndex` in `profileStore.ts` is the
   highest derived number plus one. Remove the newest derived profile and tap Generate, and the new profile has the
   same Nostr key and the same wallet seed as the removed one, over a new wallet database whose counters start at
   zero and with no restore run (the removal forgets `walletDatabaseCreated` on purpose). At a mint the old profile
   used, the wallet would derive secrets it has already used.
2. **A removed profile in the middle cannot be added back.** Generate only ever takes the next number, so the
   confirmation's "can be added again from your recovery phrase" was not true for it.
3. **The wording did not say what goes.** "Local wallet records, messages and settings" covered the payment
   history, the mint list, the keys, Whitenoise and Routstr state, and did not say that ecash is never deleted
   because removal is refused while any is held.

The owner also wants it hard to find: in Settings, not in the profile switcher, with no extra step when switching.

## Done when

Removal is offered again only from Settings, under the same lock and disk guard as a switch; a derived account number is never given to a new profile after a
removal, or adding it back runs a restore from the mints first (a record of removed numbers is new saved data, so
`persistence-release-safety` applies); the confirmation lists what is deleted and what is kept; and a native
scenario enters and cancels it on both platforms.
