The amount screen is a calculator ([ADR 0028](../../app/docs/adr/0028-the-amount-screen-is-a-calculator.md)): the amount under the navigation bar with an `OFFLINE` mark in place of the header icons, a function column (delete, swap, `MAX`, notes), the recipient's domain claim hung under the name band, and a note picker route with a pinned total and next-amount steps (`app/notes.tsx`, canonical page `note-picker`). Seen on an Android emulator in mock mode through Receive → Fixed Amount with stand-in status and picker data, because mock mode cannot open a send; the recipient column has not been seen at all. A payment request with no transport is treated as handed over directly though the wallet has no hand-over for it. Not seen on iPhone, not on a funded send, and no scenario enters the picker. `send.cashu.header-lock` and `nearby.select-from-send` were re-pointed at the flag ids and not run. `MAX` is not fee-aware: "as Lightning" after `MAX` forwards the whole balance.

## Done when

On iPhone and Android with funds: the offline mark follows a typed amount, a lock and a payment request; the note strip scrolls edge to edge; the picker opens over the flow, picks, and returns the total; both re-pointed scenarios pass and one enters the picker. A fee-aware Lightning maximum (quote, subtract reserve and input fees, re-quote until stable) replaces the forwarded balance
