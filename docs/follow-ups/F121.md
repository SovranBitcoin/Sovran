---
id: F121
title: "A notifications page whose events were all left out ends paging for that tier"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

Events on a nagg page are parsed one by one, and one that is malformed or over a ceiling is left out. When every
triggering event of a notifications page is left out and the backend says there is more, `bundleFromNotifications`
in `nostr/src/facade/notifications.ts` has no last node, returns a null cursor, and
`nostr/src/facade/session/notifications-session.ts` marks the tier exhausted, so older notifications from that
tier are never asked for. The app adapter does the same with `paginationUntil` in
`app/features/feed/data/naggFeedClient.ts`. The DM pager had the same fault and now counts what the backend sent
(`received` on the DM bundle).

Left on 2026-10-12 because the cursor has to come from the envelope's own entries, and an entry carries no time of
its own except through `actors[].createdAt`: a recorded nagg notifications response is needed to choose what the
cursor is built from.

## Done when

A notifications page with entries, all of whose events were left out, and `hasNext` true yields a cursor that asks
for the page after it; a test builds that page from an envelope.
