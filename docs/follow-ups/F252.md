The Search tab (`UnifiedSearch`, `SearchResultRows`) replaced its scope tabs with one sectioned
list: the paste-to-pay action card (`DetectedActionRow` with the `search-action` prefix), People
rows (opening the contact's profile), Mints (the account's first) and Nearby
(the Bluetooth row, which no longer needs the location). The change was proved with unit and
hook tests only; it was written in a session with no simulator or phone, so nobody has looked at
it running, and no JSON native scenario enters Search, types, and leaves.

iOS's `Stack.SearchBar` takes no `testID`; the harness reaches it by its placeholder ("People,
mints or places"). Android's field keeps `search-input`.

## Done when

Screenshots on iPhone and Android show, beside the Wallet and Settings tabs: a one-letter query
with local people and mints, a pasted ecash token with its action card, long and emoji person
names without overflow, and "bluetooth" with location denied. A scenario in
`app/e2e` opens Search, types a query, waits for a `search-section-*` row and leaves.
