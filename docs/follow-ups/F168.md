priority: high
needs: decision

`Controller.verify` in `harness/src/controller.ts` runs the historical upgrade suite for every
application or wallet change and judges it with `inspectUpgradeEvidence`
(`harness/src/upgrade.ts`). On main at `28adc886a` the suite itself passes (976 passed, 2
pending), but the inspector returns inconclusive whatever the change:

- 48 recorded known losses (`knownGaps` in `app/__tests__/releaseFixtures`), and any current
  known loss makes the gate inconclusive by design;
- 20 survival facts with no ordinary test, all wallpaper, light appearance, follower counts,
  mint changes or signer activity across epochs E7 to E19 ("Missing ordinary evidence: E7:
  keeps the wallpaper theme").

Separately, `discover-upgrade.ts` fails in a worktree whose Bitchat vendor submodules
(`app/modules/bitchat-module/*/BitChatVendor`) are not checked out:
`captureSourceFingerprint` (`app/e2e/core/git.ts`) cannot read the empty gitlink and returns
nothing, so discovery reports "Source unavailable or changed". A plain `git worktree add`
leaves them empty.

Left because whether a loss already on main may pass a task is the owner's call, and the
missing facts need tests written against the real stores.

## Done when

The owner has chosen how losses already on main are treated, the 20 facts have ordinary tests
or recorded reasons, discovery works in a worktree without its submodules (or the harness
initialises them), and a no-op app change passes the upgrade stage of `verify`.
