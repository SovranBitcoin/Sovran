---
id: F112
title: "A wallet from 0.0.61 or earlier has no keysets after upgrading until its mint answers"
priority: medium
needs: investigation
---

Opening a database written by a Coco before `@cashu/coco-expo-sqlite` 1.0.0-rc.0 (releases 0.0.45 to 0.0.61) runs
migration `015_reset_keysets_for_string_denoms`, which empties the keyset table so it is fetched again. The balance
is intact, which `cocoDatabaseUpgrade.test.ts` asserts, but until the mint is reached there are no keysets, so
anything that needs them (an offline send, ADR 0019) cannot work on first launch without a connection. Not confirmed
in the app.

From the same test: a mint quote from before Coco 2.0 that was paid but not issued stays in the legacy table and is
minted only when `reconcileLegacyMintQuotes` runs, which the app does in `enableNpcSyncAndProcessor` and which skips
untrusted mints. `docs/architecture/persistence/persistence-map.md` and the evidence ledger also omit two Coco
versions that shipped, 1.1.2-rc.32 and 1.0.0-rc.5.

## Done when

What an upgraded offline wallet can and cannot do is confirmed and either handled or stated in the app, the paid
legacy quote path is covered by a test or a device run, and the persistence documents list every Coco version in
`app/__tests__/cocoDatabases/manifest.json`.
