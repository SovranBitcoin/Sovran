title: "A paid invoice is never added when the wallet's counters are behind what the mint has signed"
priority: high
needs: investigation
---

Seen on 2026-10-13 on an iPhone simulator against `testnut.cashu.space`, three times, while writing the end-to-end
story `app/e2e/stories/library/receive-100-sats-on-testnut.json`. A wallet whose files had been put back to an
earlier moment (the runner restoring a saved state, same seed) asked for 100 sats. The mint marked the invoice paid
three seconds later, the wallet showed "Payment received", and the sats were never added. For the 30 seconds the
story waited, the wallet repeated, about every 300 ms:

```
coco.manager.MintOperationProcessor.mint_quote_claim_already_in_progress
coco.manager.MintOperationService.recovered_executing_mint_operation_after_failure
coco.manager.MintOperationService.mint_operation_returned_to_pending_after_recovery   error: "Invoice already paid or pending"
coco.manager.MintOperationProcessor.failed_to_check_or_claim_mint_quote                error: "Invoice already paid or pending"
```

With a new seed the same story is credited in 3.6 s, every time. The likely cause, not confirmed in the wallet
core's source: the restored wallet derives the same secrets it used before, the mint has already signed those,
and the claim is retried with the same outputs instead of moving the counter on. A person reaches the same place
by restoring a phone from an older backup, or by any loss of the counter that leaves the seed in place.

A copy of one failing run's log and video is in the session scratchpad only (`receive-claim-stuck/`); it is
reproduced by playing the receive story twice from the same saved `testnut-mint` state.

Not checked: the wallet core's handling of "outputs already signed", whether a later launch or Recover wallet
credits the payment, and what the mint's actual error was before the wallet reworded it.

Left because it is in the wallet core's claim and recovery path, which carries money and wants its own
investigation and a second opinion.

## Notes

The end-to-end runner avoids it by never playing a money story twice from the same saved state on one simulator.

## Done when

A wallet whose counters are behind the mint still ends with a paid invoice added to its balance, or tells the
person what to do, and a test holds that.
