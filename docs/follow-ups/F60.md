Ecash sent as a Nostr message is now redeemed automatically through the Nut Drop redeem queue (`app/features/payments/hooks/useDmEcashAutoRedeem.ts`, [ADR 0021](../../app/docs/adr/0021-a-build-chooses-its-modules.md)), so the `payments` edition hides DM pages. A token the queue parks (untrusted mint, or retries exhausted) is listed under "Needs your review" on the wallet home (`app/features/payments/components/MessageEcashReviewList.tsx`) and opens in the receive screen. The inbox is read page by page, up to 40 pages of 50 per pass. Covered by unit tests only: no funded device run has sent a token between two wallets, and the review row has not been seen on a device.

## Done when

A funded run on devices sends a token as a Nostr message between two wallets and the receiver's balance rises without a tap. A token from an untrusted mint appears under "Needs your review" on the wallet home and opens in the receive screen.
