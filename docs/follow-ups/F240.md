The original stale relay/profile publication findings were repaired on `surgery/decisions-1`.
Relay edits now begin with a complete read, stay in memory, and require explicit review; a changed
base during publishing requires another review. A legacy saved local proposal is preserved for
explicit comparison. Profile saves stop when newer confirmed knowledge arrives during verification
or signing. Regression tests cover these paths; native verification remains in F246.

Unchanged gaps from the independent source review:

- An imported account may hold its real list only on an unknown relay. Empty answers from every
  known/default relay do not disprove that list; first-run defaults may still supersede it when
  another client later encounters both. This differs from the owner-accepted risk for a known
  relay excluded after repeated silence.
- The existing store's direct edit actions inject defaults into an empty `source: relay` list.
  Network settings no longer calls those actions; the reviewed draft starts from the actual empty
  remote list. Decide whether to retain or change the remaining store action contract.
- A mute made before discovery retains the local override but is not automatically published
  after discovery. Moderation settings has manual Retry sync. Its acceptance continuation also
  lacks the final signer-identity recheck now used for profile and relay publication.

No repair of these remaining saved-state or publication-policy cases is authorized here. Nostr
also has no atomic conditional replacement: a remote change after the final read can race relay
acceptance. The completed review flow detects observed changes, not every concurrent client write.

contract is removed or justified, and each chosen repair has regression and native evidence.
