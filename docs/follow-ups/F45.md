`nostr/src/schemas.ts` parses relay and Nagg responses with unbounded, unvalidated fields: `id` and `pubkey` are bare `z.string()` (:29, :31) rather than 64-hex, `content` is unbounded (:32), and `tags` is `z.array(z.array(z.string()))` with no cap (:33); `routes`, `capabilities` and profile `name` likewise. The same file bounds one array at `:22` (`.max(5000)`), so the intent exists but is applied in one place. `nostr/src` has no hex primitive of its own, and cannot import the app's `NostrPubkeyHexSchema` from `protocolIds.ts` because `nostr` must not import from `app`. Flagged by `conventions-zod/hex-ids` (13 findings) and `input-size-caps` (14) across `schemas.ts`, `envelope.ts`, `facade/mint-reviews.ts`, `facade/primal/schemas.ts` and `recipes/wallpapers.ts`. Caps are a policy decision on the untrusted-input boundary — too low rejects legitimate events — so pick them deliberately rather than by reflex.

**Done on 2026-10-12.** `nostr/src/schemas.ts` has `NostrHexId` and `NOSTR_EVENT_LIMITS`, with the ceilings and
why they sit where they do written beside them. They are applied to:

- the nagg event schema (`id`, `pubkey`, `content`, `tags`);
- every event read off a relay or a Primal socket (`toFeedEvent` and `coerceTags` in `nostr/src/facade/event.ts`):
  an id or pubkey that is not 64 lowercase hex, or content over the ceiling, refuses the event;
- the envelope (`nostr/src/envelope.ts`): `order` elements, `cursor`, the notification entry and its actors (hex),
  the `pubkeys` list (hex) and the aggregates record, each read one item at a time so a bad item costs only itself;
- the capability probe (`routes`, `capabilities`), measured against the live service (17 routes, about 55
  capabilities, the longest under 50 characters), and the profile `name` and `picture`.

**Left:**

- `nostr/src/facade/mint-reviews.ts` (reviews, discover: `mintUrl`, names, descriptions, unit lists, operator keys),
  `nostr/src/facade/primal/schemas.ts` (event ids, ordering elements, profile fields, notification fields) and
  `nostr/src/recipes/wallpapers.ts` still use bare `z.string()` and `z.array()`. Bound them the same way: per item
  where the items are independent. Check Primal's against a live answer from the cache before choosing (it can be
  queried from a development machine).
- In `nostr/src/envelope.ts`, `NaggProvidersSchema` and the follow-status `edges` record have unbounded keys, and
  `NaggIdentitySchema` checks its pubkey with a regex of its own instead of `NostrHexId`.
- The app can publish what its own reader then leaves out. A repost carries the whole original as JSON
  (`app/features/feed/hooks/useNostrEngagement.ts`), so an original just under the content ceiling makes a repost
  over it. Either the publish paths refuse what `NOSTR_EVENT_LIMITS` would reject, or the ceiling for a kind that
  embeds another event allows for it.

## Done when

A hex primitive in `nostr/src` used for every id and pubkey; every relay-facing string, array and record bounded; limits chosen against real event sizes and documented; oversized input rejected at parse, not held
