`wallet/src/machine/createMachine.ts`: a throw between the unit switch and the tap loop skips the tap's
cleanup; an overtaken tap that switches the unit back overwrites a unit the person chose by hand after the reset; a
listener that throws after delivery makes `confirmPaymentRequest` reject although the delivery is recorded; the turn
is one way in and one way out but not one tagged value (two exit shapes and the generation counter remain).

it is closing, and a caller already waiting on an open can receive a wallet a concurrent close is about to dispose.
`app/README.md` and `docs/review/sweep-ledger.md` still mention the Cashu-token AI top-up that was deleted.
