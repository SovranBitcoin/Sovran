---
id: F94
title: "Retrying a failed wallet open does not restage the wallet phrase and fails until restart"
status: open
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

When opening the wallet fails and is retried, closing the failed attempt clears the staged wallet phrase and imported flag (`credentialStaging.ts`, `clearUnchangedSince`), and only the signer key is staged again. For an imported account the retry would have derived its seed on the derived chain. The wallet-open check added with F93 now refuses that open (`cashu.manager.staged_credentials_mismatch`), so the retry fails until the app is restarted instead of opening under the wrong seed.

## Done when

The retry stages the account and wallet phrase again, and a test opens an imported account after a failed first attempt.
