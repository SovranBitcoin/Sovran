Found on 2026-10-13 by the story `write-to-a-person-and-read-their-answer` (`app/e2e/stories/full`, the full
edition, iPhone simulator). The person writes to someone from `UserMessagesScreen`; the other person (the test
runner, with a key of its own) answers by NIP-17 message over the relays. The app has the answer within about a
second: it logs `payment.dm.envelopes.live` and `nostr.nip17.unwrap_gift_wrap.success` with the answer's length.
But the open chat did not change for the 120 s it was watched: no `chat.list.history_change`, no second bubble.
Going back to the profile and opening the chat again shows both messages at once (`dm.thread.first_page` with
`count: 2`).

So the live subscription feeds the ecash intake (`useDmEcashAutoRedeem`) but not the thread on screen
(`useDmThread`, which reads pages through `useDmEnvelopePages` in `app/features/payments`). Not looked into
further, and not tried on Android or a phone. The story opens the chat again to read the answer; that step is the
workaround to remove.

## Done when

The story's last three steps are replaced by one that sees the answer appear in the open chat, and it passes.
