Five surfaces re-arm or refresh on navigation focus alone, with no `AppState` `'active'` handling, for things the OS tears down while backgrounded: `useAmbientNfcArm.ts` (NFC arming, 0.93), `useNostrTierHealth.ts`, `ShareSignerScreen.tsx`, `SignerRequestsScreen.tsx`, `UserMessagesScreen.tsx`. `useFocusEffect` fires on screen focus, not on app foreground, so a screen that stays focused across a background/return never re-runs it — the UI can report armed or subscribed while the underlying resource is gone. The repo already uses `AppState` in eight places, including `nip46Engine.ts` and `nip46Transport.ts`, so the signer internals handle this while the signer screens do not. There is **no shared helper**, which is why it is applied unevenly. Flagged by `conventions-react-native/focus-not-foreground`.

**Code done on 2026-10-12; the device check is what is left.** `useForegroundFocusEffect`
(`app/shared/hooks/useForegroundFocusEffect.ts`) runs an effect while the screen is focused and the app is in the
foreground, tears it down when the app goes to the background and runs it again on return; the app switcher and
system sheets do not count as leaving. All five surfaces use it. Tested with a mocked `AppState`. Not seen on a
phone: NFC arming on Android after a trip to the home screen and back, and a conversation refreshing on return.

`ShareSignerScreen.tsx` was moved to the helper and moved back on 2026-10-12. Its link is usually pasted into
another app on the same phone: with the helper, leaving for that app turned off the service that answers the
connection and replaced the link on return. It stays on screen focus; the signer engine handles the foreground
itself (`nip46Engine.ts`). On a phone, check that pairing still completes after pasting the link in another app
and coming back.
