Found on 2026-10-13 by the story `a-lightning-payment-the-mint-has-not-settled` (`app/e2e/stories/library`) on the
iPhone simulator. The run's own mint is told to report the payment as pending and not answer, which is what a mint
does while a Lightning payment is in flight. After Pay on `LightningSendScreen`, for as long as it was watched
(40 s):

- The notice at the top (`payment-status-melt-processing`) is titled "Payment sent" with "Sending..." under it. It
  has not been sent.
- The timeline on the same page still says "Ready to send" and "Tap Send to complete payment", while the button
  says "Sending..." and is disabled.
- The notice stays, and it covers the header's back button (`flow-header-back`): a tap there reaches the notice,
  so the person cannot leave the page. Details is disabled too.
- The balance on the mint row has already gone from 100 to 90.

Not looked into: what the app does when the mint later answers paid or failed, after the app is closed and opened
again while pending, or on Android. Sibling wallets were not surveyed.

## Done when

The owner has chosen what a person should see and be able to do while a payment is pending, the page does that,
and the story checks it.
