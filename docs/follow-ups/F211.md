A rebalance that throws, is aborted by an account switch or is killed leaves its saved group `running` in
`swap-transactions-store`. `app/shared/lib/cashu/reconcileSwapLegs.ts` (about line 48) never selects a leg in
`melting` or `verifying`, so history shows the swap Pending for good. Related: a single-step retry that succeeds
after Cancel updates the screen and the leg but leaves the group and toast cancelled
(`useMintRebalanceOrchestrator.ts`, about lines 319 to 428; a pass 0 test pins this as a quirk); a progress leg keeps
an old error after a later success. Traced (`.surgery/pass-2/surveys/stores.md` D2 and D6, `.surgery/pass-2/surveys/transactions-wallet-mint.md` defects 1 and
4). Left because the repair changes saved status values.

## Done when

After a restart a swap with no run behind it reaches a final state that matches its Coco operations, and the group, legs and toast agree after a retry; saved field names and versions unchanged.
