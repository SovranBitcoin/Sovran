Found comparing 0.1.3 with today. (1) Nut Drop cannot happen between a 0.1.3 phone and a newer one in either direction:
different Bluetooth service id, and the new signed handshake 0.1.3 never sends. Nothing is sent; each sees the other as
not ready. Worth a line in the release notes. (2) 0.1.3's feed reader drops a whole page when one event lacks
`content` or one count is not a number, so nagg must not send either while 0.1.3 is in use. (3) Lightning addresses at
`npubx.cash` handed out by 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 stop resolving when upstream retires that domain on 2026-12-31 (from a
comment in `app/shared/lib/cashu/npc.ts`; not checked whether any release published one in a profile). (4) A pairing
started on 0.1.3 and not finished is dropped by the upgrade, on purpose.

## Done when

The release notes carry the Nut Drop line, nagg's contract names the two constraints, and the owner has ruled on the npubx.cash addresses.
