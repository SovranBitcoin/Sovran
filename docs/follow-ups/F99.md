title: "The first-launch Routstr load is fixed in tests but has not been seen on a device"
priority: low
needs: device
---

On the first launch of an install with no account list (an upgrade from 0.0.1 to 0.0.56, and a new install), `routstr-store` could not load: its adapter files everything under the account, and `captureProfileStorageOwner` threw `Profile storage is not ready` before the keys provider had added account 0. Fixed in tests on 2026-10-11: with no account the adapter reports nothing stored and writes nothing, and the store loads again as soon as account 0 has its row (`app/shared/lib/routstr/securePersistence.ts`, `onFirstAccount`). The release fixtures check Routstr on the first launch of E1 to E11, and `app/__tests__/routstrFirstLaunch.test.ts` covers the new install. What is left is a device, where the order of module evaluation and key loading is the real one.

## Done when

A first launch of a new install on a device shows no `store.routstr.rehydrate_failed` or `write_refused` in its log, and a key paid for in that session is still there after a restart.
