title: "Six link schemes the app registers do nothing, and a signer link also raises a failed-link error"
priority: low
needs: device
---

`app.json` registers `lightning`, `lnurl`, `lnurlw`, `lnurlp`, `bitcoin` and `nostr`, but the only link
consumer accepts `cashu` and `sovran`, so tapping a Lightning invoice in another app opens Sovran with no payment.
Separately, `sovran://connect?uri=…` is rewritten for the router while the wallet provider still sees the raw link,
scans the host `connect` as a payment and raises the failed-link popup beside the signer sheet. Both traced in the
source, not run.

## Done when

Each registered scheme starts its flow or is no longer registered, and a signer link raises no error. Seen on a device.
