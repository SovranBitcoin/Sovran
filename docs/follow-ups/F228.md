Pass 2 changed `app/features/send/lib/runTapToPayScan.ts` and `app/shared/lib/nfc/reader.ts` so that the scan
source's own sheet close after a successful read no longer cancels the tag session (before, the write-back on an
explicit Tap to pay failed and was rolled back, traced through the native module, never run). It is proven only in
Jest against a mocked native module. Known gap from that change: if a read succeeds but the machine drops the scan
because it is busy, nobody releases the session. Also unrun on a device: one owner for the reader
(`idle | ambient | explicit`), the single release of a tap's session and unit, an overtaken tap's rollback. A reset
during the tag write itself cannot stop the write; a token that reached the tag counts as delivered.

## Done when

A contactless payment-request pay completes on Android and iOS with the explicit and ambient flows, and the busy-drop case releases its session.
