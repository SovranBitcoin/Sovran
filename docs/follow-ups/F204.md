`app/features/composer/publish/useComposerActions.ts` (about lines 62 to 76) publishes a poll written as a reply
without the parent's tags, and drops the poll's media; no own-content record is kept. `ThreadReplyBar.tsx` (about
lines 213 to 230) shows nothing when a reply fails, and `useThread` drops a failed load-more silently. Typed
whitespace is published as is. Pinned in `app/__tests__/publishPoll.test.tsx`, `threadReplyPost.test.tsx`,
`composerPost.test.tsx` and `app/features/feed/__tests__/useThreadPaging.test.ts`. Left because the poll change
alters what is published.

## Done when

A reply poll carries its parent's tags and its media, and a failed reply or page says so.
