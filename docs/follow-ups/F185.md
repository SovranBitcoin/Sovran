---
id: F185
title: "Feed: refresh can latch, and failed replies and poll votes are silent"
priority: medium
needs: fix
---

In `HomeFeed`, pull-to-refresh followed by a switch to a recently loaded tab leaves `isRefreshing` true, which blocks
further refresh and load-more; `loadError` goes stale the same way. `ThreadReplyBar` shows nothing when a reply fails
to upload, sign or publish (the full composer does). `PollCard` ignores the vote publish result. A poll added from the
reply bar is published as a top-level poll with no reply tags (a wire-format matter, for the owner). None of these
paths has a test.

## Done when

Each path is pinned, the refresh flags clear on a tab switch, and a failed reply or vote is shown.
