title: "Switch and Connect has not been run on a phone since the pairing link moved to secure storage"
priority: low
needs: device

`app/features/nostrSigner/lib/pairingIntentStorage.ts` now keeps the pairing link for a Switch & Connect in secure
storage under one key (commit 67e38b085, then the audit fixes after it). It is covered by unit tests with a mocked
store only. Two things a phone has to answer:

- The link may be 4,096 characters, and the saved value is a little larger. Whether one secure-storage item of
  that size is accepted on both platforms is not known; the app's own vault splits values into 512-character
  items. If it is refused, Switch & Connect says the link was not saved and nothing is left behind, so the person
  has to switch and scan again.
- On iOS the keychain outlives an uninstall, so a link saved within its ten minutes survives a reinstall and is
  offered to the same account.

## Done when

Switch & Connect is run on an iPhone and an Android phone with an ordinary link and with one near the length
limit, and either works or the limit is lowered to what the phone accepts.
