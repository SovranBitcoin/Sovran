title: "Nostr cache database, restore subscription and Whitenoise are not closed on unmount"
priority: medium
needs: device
---

Nothing closes the Nostr cache database: `NostrNDKProvider` constructs the SQLite adapter and teardown only drops the reference. The adapter exposes `db.closeAsync()`, but closing under its buffered writes is untested on a device, so it was not added blind. `awaitRestoreReady` keeps its subscription when `CocoProvider` unmounts during a pending restore. With the in-process switch off, `WhitenoiseProvider` unmounts without draining calls or revoking its handle. All three only matter once an account can change without a restart.

## Done when

Each has an owner that closes, unsubscribes or drains on unmount, checked with an in-process switch on a device.
