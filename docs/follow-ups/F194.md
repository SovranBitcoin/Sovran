title: "A token copied as an emoji cannot be pasted back for 212 of the picker's emojis"
priority: medium
needs: decision
---

`app/shared/lib/third-party/emoji.ts` hides the token in variation selectors after the emoji. An emoji that itself
ends in U+FE0F (212 of the picker's 1,341, the shield and the red heart among them) makes the decoder read that as a
first byte, so the text starts with U+000F and is not recognised as a token. Same in 0.1.3. The sender's ecash stays
pending until they reclaim it. Pinned with the real codec in `app/__tests__/emojiTokenRoundTrip.test.ts`. Left
because it changes what the decoder accepts, and other wallets' decoders should be checked before the encoder
changes what it emits.

## Done when

Every emoji the picker offers round-trips a token, and tokens already sent with those emojis can be pasted.
