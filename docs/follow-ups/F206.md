Pinned in `app/__tests__/whitenoiseDmSend.test.tsx` and `whitenoiseInboxRequests.test.tsx`. A failed invite leaves
the group it just created with nothing pointing at it, and a retry creates a second group
(`useWhitenoiseDM.ts`, about lines 316 to 320). A failed send deletes the typed text. If the DM-index write fails
after a join, the request stays and accepting again joins again (`useWhitenoiseRequests.ts`, about lines 130 to
137). A decline tapped while an accept runs is not blocked.

## Done when

A failed step leaves nothing orphaned, the typed text survives, and a request cannot be accepted twice.
