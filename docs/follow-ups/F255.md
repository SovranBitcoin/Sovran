Two parts of the "final audit and UI council polish" commit were not finished.

1. `SettingsMediaScreen` (`app/features/settings/screens/SettingsMediaScreen.tsx`) still renders
   every saved image row inside `ScreenScrollView`, grouped in one `Surface` per section
   (`MediaSection`). Virtualizing them means turning the page into one `List` and splitting each
   `Surface` group into per-row pieces, which changes how the glass card looks. That needs
   someone to look at it running, so it was left.
2. The visual changes in that commit were checked by tests and type-check only, never looked at
   on a phone: Mint Info pills and status-dot entrance (`MintInfoScreen`), `StatsGrid` at large
   text, the account drawer's options button and 200% text (`AccountSwitcherDrawer`), the
   Android search field's clear button and left-aligned, wider title (`SearchLayout`,
   `GlassSearchBar.android`), the People-section error row (`SearchResultRows`), the tab bar at
   large text with the new outline/filled icons (`SovranTabBar`, `(tabs)/_layout`), and the
   full-row switches on `SettingsNetworkScreen`.

## Done when

The Uploaded media rows render through `List` with the section grouping kept, and every surface
listed above has been looked at on iPhone and Android, at default and largest text size, with
anything out of place fixed.
